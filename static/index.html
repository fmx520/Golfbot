How It Works

How does Golfbot know my course's booking link?

The links for the supported courses are built into Golfbot, so you just pick your course from the dropdown. If you need a course that isn't listed, choose Other — paste a ForeUp URL and paste the booking page URL from your browser's address bar (it looks like https://foreupsoftware.com/index.php/booking/19765/2431#/teetimes). Golfbot pulls the Schedule ID and Course ID out of it automatically.

What is the link check on the Connect page?

Before you book, Golfbot loads each built-in course's booking page straight from ForeUp and confirms that ForeUp still recognises the schedule and course IDs in the link — and it shows you the course name ForeUp reports back, so you can see you're pointed at the right place. A stale link would otherwise look completely normal until the bot searched an empty tee sheet, or raced a release on the wrong course.

Results are cached for 15 minutes; Re-check forces a fresh check. If ForeUp doesn't answer or serves a bot check, Golfbot says it couldn't confirm the link rather than claiming it's broken — you can still connect. Only a link ForeUp actively rejects blocks the Connect button.

What is Dry Run?

Dry Run searches for tee times but does not book anything. Use it to preview availability and confirm your filters are correct before committing to a real booking.

What is Sniper Mode?

Sniper Mode is designed to beat other bots when courses release tee times at a fixed time (e.g., 7:00 PM). It pre-authenticates your session, then at release time polls the ForeUp API every 150–300ms until a matching slot appears, and books within milliseconds of the slot showing up. The 90-second timeout stops polling if nothing is released.

Sniper is also the right choice for a mid-day cancellation — someone drops a Saturday slot at 2pm and you want to grab it. That is what Start now is for: no release to wait on, just poll until something appears.

What is Future Sight, and how is it different from Sniper?

Sniper does two things at the release instant: it asks ForeUp what opened, then it books. Future Sight skips the asking. It works out the exact tee times your course will publish before the release, then fires holds directly at those times the moment the sheet opens — no discovery round trip, straight to the front of the queue.

That round trip is roughly 300ms, and a release is decided in less than that. Everything else about the race is work ForeUp does on its side that everyone pays equally; the lookup is the part we can simply not do.

It holds up to six times one at a time, in order, and stops at the first one accepted. If they all miss it re-fires for a few seconds in case the sheet published late, then falls back to normal polling — so a wrong guess costs a little time, never the race.

Because the holds are real, Future Sight requires Auto-book on and Dry Run off, and it needs a release time. Sniper and Future Sight are either/or: they are different tools, not two settings you stack.

Why can't I use "Start now" with Future Sight?

Pre-staging only beats polling when the times are known in advance. At a release, every time opens at once, so your whole window is worth aiming at. Started mid-day, exactly one slot has opened — somebody's cancellation — and nobody knows which. Firing six holds to find out is a shotgun in the dark, and a single poll would just tell you. Worse, the burst would delay the polling that was going to win.

So for a cancellation, pick Sniper with Start now. That is the tool built for it.

How does Future Sight know the times before they're published?

Courses run tee times on a fixed grid — Bethpage Red, for example, goes every 9 minutes anchored on :03, so 7:06, 7:15, 7:24 and so on. 7:10 does not exist there and a hold aimed at it fails no matter how fast it is.

Golfbot works the grid out by reading a day whose sheet is already published, preferring the same weekday a week earlier, and then intersects it with the time window you asked for. You never type a time in: you say "7:00 to 8:00" and it turns that into the real slots.

What it learns is remembered per course and weekday, so a day when the reference sheet is booked solid no longer means falling back to guessing. Pressing Show this course's tee grid for a date with plenty of open times is the fastest way to teach it a new course — and any release Golfbot polls gets banked automatically.

One caveat worth knowing: if the only times it could see were, say, twilight slots, the grid for your 7am window is extrapolated from those. It is usually right, because most courses use one interval all day — but that is the assumption, and off-grid targets are simply rejected and fall through to polling.

Several of us are booking the same course. Do we fight each other?

Not any more. Golfbot ranks slots the same way every time, so without this, everyone armed on the same course would pick the identical time and all but one would lose — a loss that looks exactly like losing to an outside bot.

When you arm, Golfbot checks whether other jobs are already racing identical criteria — same courses, date, booking class, players and holes — and rotates your pick order so each of you opens on a different time. Nobody loses a chance: every job still tries your whole window and still falls through to whatever is left. It only changes who goes first. You'll see a note saying you're #2 of 3 when it applies.

It only coordinates on an exact match. A different booking class sees different inventory, so steering you off a slot could point you at a time that does not exist for you. And if you are the only one armed, nothing changes at all.

What is Auto-Book?

When enabled alongside Sniper Mode, the bot will automatically submit the booking the instant a matching slot is found — no manual intervention needed. Future Sight always books, so it switches Auto-book on and locks it there.

If your course emails a booking code, the run pauses and asks you for it, keeping the hold alive while it waits. You have about two minutes.

What is the Booking Class?

ForeUp courses offer different booking classes (e.g., Resident, Non Resident, Verified NYS Resident) with different pricing and availability. After connecting, the dropdown populates with the options for your course. Pick the one that matches your status to see the right times and prices. "Auto-detect" tries unprotected classes first.

What does the Release Time mean?

This is the time your course publishes new tee times — for example, many NY state courses release at 7:00 PM for the following week. Set this to match your course's release schedule. Golfbot arms early, re-warms your session shortly before, and starts racing at exactly this time.

Arm a few minutes ahead, not hours. A couple of minutes is plenty to get set up, and leaving a job counting down all afternoon mostly just gives it more time to be interrupted.

Race timeline

At arming — Authenticate, pre-solve CAPTCHA, and (Future Sight) work out the target times
T-12s — Re-warm session (fresh cookies + JWT)
T-0s — Future Sight fires its holds; Sniper starts polling every 150–300ms
T+match — Slot taken → book instantly or alert
T+90s — Timeout if nothing was released

The re-warm used to sit at T-5s, which was not enough room: when it ran long it overran the release and polling silently started late. It now has real headroom, and if it ever does overrun, the run says so plainly instead of looking like an ordinary loss.
